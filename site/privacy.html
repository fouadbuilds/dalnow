Privacy policy

Your Brightspace information stays in your browser.

DalNow is built to give Dalhousie students a clearer deadline list without asking for a password, account, or personal profile.

What DalNow reads

DalNow reads course and deadline information from the Brightspace session already signed in to your browser. It uses that information only to show your deadlines and send the reminders you choose.

What stays local

Your deadline list, course preferences, reminder settings, completed items, and theme preference are stored in Chrome on your device. DalNow does not ask for or store your Brightspace password.

What we collect

DalNow does not include analytics, advertising, or a user account. We do not collect, sell, or share your Brightspace information.

Delete your data

Select Delete my data in DalNow’s settings to remove its locally saved deadlines and preferences from your browser.
