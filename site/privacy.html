DalNow

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

DalNow helps Dalhousie students see Brightspace deadlines in one place. It is designed to work without an account, password, analytics, or advertising.

Information DalNow uses

When you are signed in to Dalhousie Brightspace, DalNow reads the course and deadline information needed to build your deadline list. This can include course names, assignment, quiz, discussion, and calendar deadlines, availability dates, and whether an item has been completed.

DalNow uses your existing Brightspace browser session. It does not ask for, read, or store your Brightspace password.

Where your information stays

Your deadline list, course preferences, reminder settings, completed items, and theme preference are stored locally in Chrome on your device. DalNow does not create a personal profile or sync this information to a DalNow server.

Information we collect

DalNow does not include analytics, advertising, or a user account. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your Brightspace information.

DalNow only communicates with Dalhousie Brightspace when it checks for your deadlines. Those requests use the browser session you already have with Brightspace.

Reminders and notifications

If you turn on reminders, DalNow uses Chrome notifications on your device to show them. Reminder timing and the record of reminders already sent stay local to your browser.

Delete your data

Select Delete my data in DalNow’s settings to clear the information and preferences that DalNow has saved locally in your browser. Removing the extension also removes its local storage.

Changes to this policy

If DalNow’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change too.
